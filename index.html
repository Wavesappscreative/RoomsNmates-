Questions? Get in touch ↗
Is RoomsNmates free?

Browsing and posting one room are free. Both room hosts and room seekers need an active Premium subscription to read incoming messages. Messaging limits apply. Review current pricing and renewal terms in Google Play before purchasing.

Can I download it right now?

Yes. RoomsNmates is publicly available on Android through Google Play. Tap the Google Play button to open the listing and download the app. RoomsNmates is for adults 18 and over.

Who is the app for?

RoomsNmates is for adults 18 and over looking for rooms or potential roommates, or posting a room to share.

Are people and properties verified?

Profiles and listings are member-provided. RoomsNmates does not perform background checks or guarantee identity, age, property ownership, or safety. Independently verify the property, lease, and person before meeting or paying. You can report concerns and block users in the app. New and edited room listings require approval.

How do I manage Premium?

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription through Google Play. Manage or cancel it in Google Play. Deleting your RoomsNmates account does not cancel your subscription.
