OUR COMMITMENT

Accessibility at RoomsNmates

RoomsNmates should work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, magnification, or other assistive technology.

01

What we are working toward.

We are improving the RoomsNmates Android app and this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA — the standard used in accessibility agreements and court settlements. This is an ongoing effort, and we prioritize fixes that block people from using the app.

02

Screen readers on Android.

The RoomsNmates app runs on Android. You can use it with TalkBack, Android's built-in screen reader, and with Android's display and font-size settings. We label buttons, images, and form fields so screen readers can describe them, and we keep improving those labels.

03

Tell us about a problem.

If any part of the app or this website is hard or impossible to use with your assistive technology, email us at drivfiappscreative@gmail.com. Describe what you were trying to do and what went wrong. We aim to respond within 2 business days and to prioritize fixes that block access.
